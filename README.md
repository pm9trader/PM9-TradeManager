# PM9 Trade Manager for MetaTrader 5

### Advanced Position Management. Complete Trading Control.

PM9 Trade Manager is a professional trade management tool for MetaTrader 5, designed for scalpers, day traders, and prop traders who demand speed, precision, and flexibility.

Built from real trading experience, PM9 brings essential trade management features together in one intuitive, responsive, and customizable panel.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Fast Position Management** – Open, manage, and close positions quickly and conveniently.
- **Stop Loss & Take Profit** – Set and adjust SL and TP levels with ease.
- **Break-Even Management** – Move positions to break-even when needed.
- **Partial Close** – Close half or a selected portion of a position.
- **Advanced Pending Orders** – Manage Buy and Sell pending orders.
- **Daily Drawdown Monitor** – Set a custom daily drawdown percentage and monitor your account's daily risk limit.
- **Professional Statistics** – Access daily and overall trading statistics and reports.
- **Position Duration** – Monitor how long each position has been open.
- **Prop Trading Tools** – Features designed to help traders monitor important account limits.
- **Audio Notifications** – Customize sounds for different trading events, including position openings, Take Profit, and Stop Loss.
- **Chart Management** – Hide trend lines to keep your chart clean and readable.
- **Flexible Interface** – Move and customize the panel to suit your trading workspace.
